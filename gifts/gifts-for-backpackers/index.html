FAQ

What is a good gift for someone who loves backpacking?

Lightweight upgrades such as merino socks, an inflatable pillow, a water filter, a rechargeable headlamp, or an ultralight stove.

What should I avoid buying a backpacker?

Heavy novelty gadgets and sized items like boots or packs. Fit is personal, and they'd rather choose those themselves.

Is the Garmin inReach Mini 2 a good gift?

It can suit people who travel beyond cell coverage. It needs an active paid Garmin subscription to send messages or SOS, and it doesn't replace trip planning or telling someone your route.
