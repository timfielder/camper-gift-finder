FAQ

What is a good gift for someone who loves backpacking?

Lightweight upgrades they'll carry every trip: merino socks, an inflatable pillow, a water filter, a rechargeable headlamp, or an ultralight stove.

What should I avoid buying a backpacker?

Heavy novelty gadgets and sized items like boots or packs. Fit is personal, and they'd rather choose those themselves.

Is the Garmin inReach Mini 2 worth it as a gift?

It's a great safety gift for people who go beyond cell coverage, but it needs a paid satellite plan to send messages and SOS.
