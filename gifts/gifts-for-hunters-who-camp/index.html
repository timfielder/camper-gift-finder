FAQ

What do you get a hunter who has everything?

Consumables and upgrades they may not buy for themselves: game bags, hand and toe warmers, a jerky cannon, a wild game cookbook, or better glass like Vortex binoculars.

What's a good gift for a hunter under $25?

The Primos Original Can deer call, Koola Buck game bags, HotHands warmers, a Carhartt beanie, or The MeatEater Fish and Game Cookbook.

Can a Mr. Heater Buddy be used in a tent or blind?

Mr. Heater's manual approves it for indoor use in small recreational enclosures that have ventilation, such as tents, blinds, and ice shanties, and it lists a low-oxygen sensor and tip-over shutoff. The same manual says never operate it while sleeping or leave it unattended. Follow the manual's ventilation and clearance rules exactly and use a working carbon monoxide alarm.

Do I need to know their gear sizes?

No. Nothing on this list needs a clothing or boot size, which makes it easier to give.
