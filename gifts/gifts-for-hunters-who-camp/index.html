FAQ

What do you get a hunter who has everything?

Consumables and upgrades they won't buy themselves: game bags, hand and toe warmers, a jerky cannon, a wild game cookbook, or better glass like Vortex binoculars.

What's a good gift for a hunter under $25?

The Primos Original Can deer call, Koola Buck game bags, HotHands warmers, a Carhartt beanie, or The MeatEater Fish and Game Cookbook.

Is a Mr. Heater Buddy safe in a tent or blind?

It is rated for indoor use with an oxygen-depletion sensor and tip-over shutoff, but always follow the ventilation instructions and use a carbon monoxide alarm.

Do I need to know their gear sizes?

No. Everything on this list is one-size or non-sized, which makes it safer as a gift.
