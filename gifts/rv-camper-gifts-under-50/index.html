FAQ

What is a good gift for a new RV owner?

A water pressure regulator, a drinking-water hose, and inline water filters. A regulator limits incoming campground water pressure to help protect the RV's plumbing.

What are practical gifts for RVers?

Spare water filters, campsite lighting, and campfire cooking gear like a pie iron or cast iron skillet. For CO or propane alarms, choose a model its maker approves for RV use.

Are these gifts for motorhomes or travel trailers?

Both. The water accessories use standard garden-hose threads found on most RV fresh-water inlets, but check the RV's connections before you buy.
