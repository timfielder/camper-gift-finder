FAQ

What is a good gift for a new RV owner?

A water pressure regulator, a drinking-water-safe hose, and inline water filters. New RVers often don't have them and they protect the plumbing.

What do RVers need that they won't buy themselves?

Spare filters, a carbon monoxide alarm, better campsite lighting, and campfire cooking gear like a pie iron or cast iron skillet.

Are these gifts for motorhomes or travel trailers?

Both. The water accessories fit standard RV hookups on motorhomes, fifth wheels, and travel trailers.
