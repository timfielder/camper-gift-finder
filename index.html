What's a good gift for someone who likes camping?

What are good camping gifts under $25?

Headlamps (Petzl Tikkina), HotHands warmers, UCO stormproof matches, a LifeStraw filter straw, a Lodge 6.5-inch skillet, merino beanies, and a Night Sky star finder.

What do you get a camper who has everything?

Consumables and upgrades: hand warmers, fuel-free fire starters, better socks, a camp cookbook, or an experience like a printable camping trip voucher.
